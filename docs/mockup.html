The model

How the Blue Moon score works

  • The Blue Moon score answers one question: is this owner worth chasing?
  • It works like a weather forecast. Start rare, then each signal pushes the odds up or down.
1.5%
Start rare

Before we know anything, about 1 in 70 properties in the buy box could be bought at a Blue Moon price.

×
Each signal multiplies

A 25-year owner doubles the odds (×2.0). An institutional owner cuts them sharply (×0.15).

—
Missing means skipped

No data on a signal? It's left out, not counted against the property. The bars show which ones were skipped.

Data shows certainty

The Data column shows how many of the 10 signals we have. A 12% on 9 of 10 is firmer than a 12% on 4 of 10.

Starting weights

These are starting estimates from common investor practice for finding motivated sellers. They are labeled as estimates until your own deals confirm or correct them.

Weights adjusted
Not yet. After about 30 outcomes, weights start shifting toward what actually happened.

Every owner you reach, and every deal that later works out in your CRM, is evidence. The model compares the signals on owners who sold against ones who didn't, and nudges each weight.

What it can't see yet

Unpaid property taxes, code violations, probate and evictions are strong signs a seller may take a low price. Counties publish some of these. They're good candidates for a later layer.

Roadmap

What gets built, in order

  • Goal: find the owner and confirm we can reach them.
  • Conversations happen in your CRM. Pricing comes later, separately.
1

Foundation

  • Import Crexi files into one master list; merge duplicates
  • Brad's buy box: 10–80 units, sweet spot 20–50, within 60 minutes, built 1970+
  • Size from units + sq ft, then units, then sq ft
  • Drive time, HUD rent fit, ranked list to Google Sheets
2

DRILL and contact levels

  • Levels 1–3 per property: no owner data, possible owner, confirmed owner
  • Channel badges for mail, phone, text, email, social and voicemail drop
  • Free sources first: Ohio Secretary of State, county auditor and recorder, web search
  • Spend all 500 Crexi credits each month on the top-ranked owners not yet confirmed
  • Penetration dashboard and a drill timeline on every property
  • Do Not Call flags on phone numbers
3

Walk-up photo check

  • Street View and satellite photos read by AI on your Claude plan
  • Review queue for unsure results; your answers become examples
4

Learning loops

  • Hit rate of each source by owner type, so the drill tries the best source first
  • Blue Moon score weights tuned from real results and past deals
5

Owner profiles and archetypes

  • A profile of the people behind each property, built from everything gathered
  • Archetypes with guesses about their situation, what they value, and how to message them
  • Starting archetypes: long-time local owner, distant heirs, tired self-manager, small portfolio operator, institution
  • Only property, business and public-record facts. No guesses about health, religion, ethnicity or other personal traits
+

Later

  • Blue Moon strike price from Brad's spreadsheet (separate buildout)
  • More buy boxes for wholesale buyers
  • Distress data: tax delinquency, code violations
